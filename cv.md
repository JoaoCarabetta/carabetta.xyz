# joão carabetta — CV

physicist by training. builds public data infrastructure in Brazil — open datasets, city platforms, and the tools between them.

## Experience

### CTO of Rio · President of iplanrio

City of Rio de Janeiro / iplanrio · 2025-01 → 2026-04

Led the municipal technology company (founded 1979) responsible for the systems the city runs on — 1746, Carioca Digital, processo.rio, health systems.

- Scaled proactive AI / digital outreach against multidimensional vulnerability — Bloomberg Mayors Challenge winner (US$1M).
- Supported city-scale products including Histórico Clínico Integrado (~7M patients) and Mulher.Rio.

### Chief Data Officer · Escritório de Dados

Mayor's Cabinet, City of Rio de Janeiro · 2021-11 → 2025-01

Founded and ran Rio's Data Office — research, training, and open storytelling at dados.rio.

- Launched the municipal public datalake (2022): 20+ secretariats, 4.7B+ records — Google Cloud global public-sector case.
- Fed CIVITAS (city intelligence hub) and operational dashboards managers actually use.
- Advanced from Head of Data Science (transport) → CDO → Chief Executive of Technology → President of iplanrio.

### Head of Data Science — Transport

SMTR, City of Rio de Janeiro · 2021-02 → 2021-11

Built the live bus GPS pipeline — every ping, line, and operator — so the city could measure service delivery in real time. Seed of the municipal data program.

### Co-founder

Base dos Dados · 2020-09 →

Public data as infrastructure: one cleaned, joinable warehouse on BigQuery — free at the point of use.

- Google Cloud Next'21 Social Impact Award (only Brazilian org in the category) and 1st place, Prêmio Tesouro Nacional.
- Standardized identifiers so Brazil's public tables join — search, lake, and APIs for researchers and journalists.

### Data Scientist

Inter-American Development Bank (IDB) · 2019-09 → 2021-01

Strategic planning & development effectiveness — novel data sources as evidence for policy across Latin America. Built a continent-wide Waze pipeline for mobility, pollution, and transit-cost indexes (~40 largest cities), plus the COVID impact dashboard and urban air-quality work.

### Data Scientist

Gabinete Compartilhado Acredito — Brazilian Congress · 2019-02 → 2019-05

Data support for deputies Tábata Amaral, Felipe Rigoni and senator Alessandro Vieira — built a framework to live-capture and process political data at scale.

### In-company professor

FGV — Rio de Janeiro · 2018-10 → 2019-02

Taught Data Visualisation and Introduction to Data Modelling with Tableau.

### Project Manager → Project Head

Cognitivo.ai — Rio de Janeiro · 2018-04 → 2019-06

Led data projects at a Rio analytics startup before moving into public-interest work full time.

- Owned project resources, people allocation, infrastructure, and customer satisfaction.

### Data Scientist

CTS-FGV — Rio de Janeiro · 2017-02 → 2019-02

Analytics and infrastructure for Congresso em Números and the IDB Big Data for Sustainable Development project.

### Data Analyst residency

LabHacker — São Paulo · 2016-09 → 2016-12

Data analyst residency at Pedro Markun's tech×politics organization — built Agenda Pública, an app gathering popular deliberative meetings across São Paulo.
